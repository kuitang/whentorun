WBGT — wet-bulb globe temperature

Measures — the combined heat load of temperature, humidity, direct sun, and wind: the closest single number to what running in heat actually costs.

Leaves out — your acclimation, fitness, and pace.

The words — under 80 °F elevated at most; 80–85 moderate heat stress; 85–88 high heat stress; 88–90 extreme caution; 90 and up we simply say not to run.

Authority — National Weather Service heat-stress bands.

Dew point

Measures — the moisture in the air, and so how well sweat can evaporate. Above 70 °F, it mostly cannot.

The words — under 55 comfortable; 55–60 noticeable; 60–65 sticky; 65–70 very humid; 70–75 oppressive; 75 and up miserable.

Authority — NWS observation and forecast fields.

UV index

Measures — sunburning ultraviolet at the surface. 0–2 low; 3–5 moderate; 6–7 high; 8–10 very high; 11+ extreme.

Leaves out — heat; a cool day can still burn.
