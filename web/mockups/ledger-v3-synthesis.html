What is WBGT?

Wet-bulb globe temperature folds air temperature, humidity, sun load, and wind into one number for how hard heat works a moving body. It is the standard the National Weather Service and OSHA use for outdoor exertion — watch it, not the plain air temperature. Two 75° mornings can run worlds apart; WBGT tells them apart.
